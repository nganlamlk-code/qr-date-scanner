HUONG DAN TIEP TUC QR DATE

1. URL Web app da duoc gan san vao index.html.
2. Dang nhap GitHub, mo repository nganlamlk-code/qr-date-scanner.
3. Mo file index.html trong repository, chon Edit (bieu tuong but chi).
4. Thay toan bo noi dung file index.html bang noi dung cua index.html trong bo nay.
5. Nhap commit message, bam Commit changes.
6. Cho GitHub Pages cap nhat, sau do mo https://nganlamlk-code.github.io/qr-date-scanner/
7. Nhap ban ghi thu, bam Gui du lieu. Kiem tra ca Form_Responses va DU LIEU DATE trong Google Sheets.

Luu y: Trang co the hien 'Da gui yeu cau' do trinh duyet dung no-cors, nhung chi xem la thanh cong khi ban ghi that su xuat hien trong Google Sheets. Neu khong co du lieu, kiem tra Apps Script > Executions (Lan thuc thi) de xem loi.

Neu GitHub Pages khong cap nhat, thu Ctrl+F5 hoac mo cua so an danh. Khong xoa DMScan neu danh muc UPC van con.
